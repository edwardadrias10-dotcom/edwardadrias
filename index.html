About Me

Bridging human needs and technical systems

I bridge the gap between technical systems and human needs. Whether I'm curating data for healthcare platforms or helping a customer troubleshoot an issue, I bring patience, clarity, and genuine care to every interaction.

My journey started in customer service at DISH Network, where I learned the art of turning frustrated callers into loyal customers. That foundation led me to Cognizant, where I managed complex campaigns, and eventually to Veeva Systems as a Data Curator.

What drives me? The satisfaction of taking something messy — whether that's a confused customer or a chaotic dataset — and bringing order to it.

When I'm not working, you'll find me exploring new tech, enjoying good coffee, or discovering the best local spots in Zamboanga City.
