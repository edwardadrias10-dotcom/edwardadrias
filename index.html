FLAGSHIP BUILD

Full Lead Lifecycle

The problem: Leads often go cold simply because no one follows up fast enough, or follows up the same way regardless of how interested the lead actually is.
The approach: I built an escalating sequence that branches based on real behavior — a reply within the first hour gets an urgent call task, no reply triggers an SMS nudge instead of silence.
Why it matters: This is the difference between a lead sitting untouched for days and getting a human response while interest is still fresh.
TESTED LIVE

Lead Engagement Speed Tiers

The problem: Not every lead deserves the same response speed — treating a hot lead and a lukewarm one identically wastes the urgency of the ones most likely to convert.
The approach: I segmented follow-up by how fast someone responds, so a quick reply routes to an immediate call while a slower one gets a next-day call instead.
Why it matters: Sales teams can focus their fastest response on the leads most likely to close, instead of spreading effort evenly.
Replied fast → Immediate call
Replied → Next-day call
No reply → Re-engagement email
TESTED LIVE

Lost Lead Cleanup

The problem: Pipelines get messy fast when "not interested" leads just sit in active stages, making reports and forecasts unreliable.
The approach: A single tag-based trigger automatically moves a contact's opportunity to Lost the moment they're marked not interested, no manual cleanup required.
Why it matters: Keeps pipeline data honest in real time, so reporting always reflects what's actually still active.
Tag: Not Interested → Opportunity → Lost
